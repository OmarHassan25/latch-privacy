Latch privacy policy

Latch is an app blocker by goapps that runs only on your phone. This is the same text the app shows under More › About › Privacy policy.

What Latch reads

Which app is open, so it can block the apps you chose; the list of apps you can launch, so you can pick them; and whether its own permissions are on, for Protection health.

What Latch stores

Your rules, essentials, strict mode state and a decision log of the last 8 days (package names and times). All of it stays in Latch's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling Latch deletes it.

What Latch sends

Nothing. Latch has no internet permission, no account, no analytics and no crash reporting. Diagnostics are a text file you can share yourself; nothing is sent unless you share it.

Payments

Latch Pro is bought through the Google Play app. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; Latch only learns whether Pro is active.
